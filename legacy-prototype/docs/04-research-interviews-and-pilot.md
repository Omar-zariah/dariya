# Customer research and pilot operations kit

## Ask property owners (without pitching your solution first)

1. Where is the home, who owns it, and who has the keys today?
2. How many months per year is it empty, rented or occupied by relatives?
3. When did you last discover an issue late because you were outside Morocco? What happened and what did it cost?
4. Who checked or repaired it? How did you know the work was complete?
5. In the last year, how often did you spend money without seeing an itemized quote/receipt? Show me the actual process (not an estimate).
6. What part felt least reliable: access, reporting, quality, speed, cost, or payment?
7. Have you paid a person/agency to inspect or manage the property? How much, how often, and why continue/stop?
8. What authorization would you be comfortable giving a third-party agent? What would be unacceptable?
9. Would you purchase a *paid*, clearly scoped one-off check for an actual home now? At what price would you decline?
10. How do you want evidence delivered, and in what language?

Recruit owners living in several host countries with properties in the *same* Moroccan launch area, including those who are satisfied with relatives' help. Avoid recruiting only people with known severe problems.

## Field inspection checklist

- Confirm address, access and authorization before entering.
- Timestamp with property ID, inspector ID, start/finish and locked checklist.
- External doors/windows, locks, roof/terrace (where safe), moisture, visible cracks, odors.
- Plumbing and water meter, electrical panel (visual only), ventilation/AC, pests and visible tampering.
- Photograph each issue with scale and location; do not claim a licensed technical inspection without qualifications.
- Escalate immediate risks to qualified responders.
- Close with checklists, original images, owner-readable summary, next suggested action.

## Pilot operating data to track (by job)

`property_id, scheduled_at, accepted_at, arrival_at, completed_at, inspector_id, travel_minutes, on_site_minutes, review_minutes, total_direct_cost_mad, report_delivered_at, complaint, repeat_visit, quote_value_mad, owner_decision, evidence_complete`

## Paid pilot scorecard

- Homes paying and homes renewed after first inspection cycle.
- Cost per acquisition, CAC payback and gross contribution after actual field/CS/payment costs.
- First-time inspection completion by promised due date; reschedule and revisit rates.
- Quote/receipt mismatch and number of repairs re-opened.
- Owner trust: repeat use, referrals, complaints and observed dispute patterns.

## Trust and reliability commitments to test

Promise only services you can consistently perform with local coverage. Use transparent exclusions (after-hours, urgent emergencies, major structural safety issues). Separate a finding from a diagnosis, and a quote from a final cost. Define owner approval thresholds in writing and don't create an incentive for unnecessary maintenance.
