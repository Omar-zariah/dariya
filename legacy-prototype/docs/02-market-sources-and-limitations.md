# Morocco market context: evidence vs. assumptions

As of **3 October 2026**. Official sources linked below; these are *background indicators* and **do not independently establish the number of addressable diaspora-owned homes**.

## Public evidence

1. **Moroccan urban housing stock (2024 census):** HCP reports **8.34 million urban housing units** in 2024, of which around **5.9 million occupied**, **1.1 million vacant** and **1.3 million secondary/seasonal**. The shares are **71.1% occupied**, **13.4% vacant**, **15.5% secondary/seasonal**. These are classifications of *all urban properties*; ownership by Moroccan expatriates is not specified. Source: [Haut-Commissariat au Plan, urban housing stock (December 2025)](https://www.hcp.ma/Parc-logement-urbain-au-Maroc-selon-les-resultats-du-recensement-general-de-la-population-et-de-l-habitat-de-2024_a4226.html).
2. **MRE transfers (calendar year 2025):** Office des Changes reported **122,023 million MAD** in funds transferred by Moroccans resident abroad, compared with **118,979 million MAD** in 2024 (provisional). These are all remittances, **not** sums dedicated to property care or housing. Source: [Office des Changes, publications: IMEE 2025](https://www.oc.gov.ma/en/publications?active=2494&page=1).
3. **More recent partial-year remittances:** At **end-August 2026**, the Office des Changes reported **89,215 million MAD**, compared with **81,846 million MAD** for the equivalent January–August 2025 window (about +9%, preliminary). Source: [Office des Changes, IMEE August 2026](https://www.oc.gov.ma/fr/publications?active=2699&page=0).

## What the evidence does NOT prove

- 'Vacant' does not mean 'diaspora-owned'. Secondary residences need not be diaspora-owned.
- Remittances cannot be translated into spend on management without direct research.
- We have **no measured estimate here** of addressable verified diaspora property owners, their willingness to pay, or subscription take-up rate.
- Claims that fraud, poor maintenance, or contractor problems are common require representative survey or complaint evidence; at present they are interview hypotheses.
- Public nationwide numbers may poorly reflect the one city/zones a service operator can cover.

## Bottom-up market sizing: recommended method

For the chosen launch city:

```
Serviceable homes = homes within reachable zones
                  × fraction verified owned/authorized by people abroad
                  × fraction needing outside help
                  × fraction accepting the service scope/price

Subscription revenue = paying homes × average monthly realized fee × 12
Contribution         = revenue − field visits − support − payments −
                       verification − refunds − other direct costs
Operating profit     = contribution − marketing − fixed staff − rent −
                       software − legal/admin − insurance
```

Each multiplication factor must be traced to property records, paid pilots or credible independent surveys. Be explicit about geography and customer eligibility; never present these factors as established national facts.

## Research priorities

- Verify ownership/authorization and identify the real decision-maker.
- Ask what owners currently spend on emergency repairs and ad-hoc oversight.
- Measure actual time per inspection: drive time, photos, scheduling and escalation.
- Observe how often owners accept proposed quote amounts and whether they prefer a local employee versus independent providers.
- Quantify first-year retention and ratio of homes per inspector for each density/route.
